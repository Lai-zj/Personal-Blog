## 概念
曲线$\Gamma$为分段光滑的空间有向曲线，$\Sigma$是以$\Gamma$为边界的有向曲面（其中$\Gamma$的正向与$\Sigma$的侧符合右手规则）；P，Q，R在$\Sigma$上具有一阶连续偏导数

$$
\oint_{\Gamma} Pdx+Qdy+Rdz
=\iint_{\Sigma} (\frac{\partial R}{\partial y}-\frac{\partial Q}{\partial z})dydz
+(\frac{\partial P}{\partial z}-\frac{\partial R}{\partial x})dzdx
+(\frac{\partial Q}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial P}{\partial y})dzdx
$$
